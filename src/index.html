Hand features

Coach · this hand

Your first decision is graded by the range chart (or EV). Later decisions follow the self-play strategy: bets and raises use your equity vs. each opponent's preflop range; calls and folds use your equity vs. the hands they are likely to hold, read from their betting, against the price after rake. Reads are range-vs-range: every bet, raise, call and check reweights the hands in their range by how often the bots' own strategy (with its Nit/Maniac tilt, bluffs and traps) would take that action with each one, so a river bet still contains bluffs. Bot-vs-bot tests: these reads land within ~1–3 points of the true average equity (the old cut-off reads were 6–14 points too pessimistic). It's a heuristic coach, not a solver.

Ranges & EV model
Hand rank = simulated pot share 3-way against 2 hands from the top 40% ("hands that would actually play"), compared with 40,000 simulated random hands (5,000 runouts each). The default ranges are a fixed point of the EV model: every spot's ranges are a best response to the others, so the range chart and the EV tree agree. Change anything here and the two can drift apart. It's an equilibrium of a simplified model, not a full-game solver.
